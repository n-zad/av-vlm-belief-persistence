This is an interesting and well-focused evaluation problem, especially the stateful-vs.-stateless comparison. I recommend making the experiment more controlled: do not define every initial incorrect answer as a hallucination, since the first frame may simply be ambiguous or occluded. In addition to naturally occurring errors, construct controlled sequences where an incorrect belief is established in the conversation history and then provide progressively stronger contradictory visual evidence. You can then measure how much evidence—or how many frames—each VLM requires to correct the belief, and compare this directly with stateless inference. I would initially focus only on object presence/identity and leave spatial relationships and driving-action recommendations as extensions. Also, the available RTX 3050 Ti 4GB is unlikely to be sufficient for the proposed VLMs, so please establish an API/rented-GPU or smaller-model inference plan before scaling the evaluation.
